📥 CSV Upload & Cleaning Pipeline

Upload new cohort files or mass updates. Uploaded files automatically execute the data cleaning pipeline before updating the database.

⚙️ Automated 5-Step Cleaning & Merging Pipeline:
1. UID Normalization: Strips whitespace and forces uppercase format.
2. Duplicate Elimination: Drops duplicate entries by UID.
3. Missing Imputation: Imputes missing numerical values with cohort medians.
4. Range Clamping: Enforces domain bounds on CGPA, marks, and attendance.
5. Dynamic Scoring: Recalculates Success Scores, risk flags, and explainability.
